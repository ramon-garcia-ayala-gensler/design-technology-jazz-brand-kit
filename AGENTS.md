# DT Jazz — instructions for AI agents / instrucciones para agentes

Use this style whenever you make slides, posters, motion, web pages or documents for Design Technology
(style names: "DT Jazz", "Design Technology Jazz", "Amplify style"). Full spec: `copilot/knowledge/brand-guide.md`,
tokens in `tokens/tokens.json` and `tokens/tokens.css`, assets in `assets/`, deck example in `templates/slides/index.html`.

## Look
- Dark jazz-club line art. Background `#0B0B0D` with fine film grain (6%) and a black-only vignette. Never a gradient background.
- One graphic resource: a warm-white line `#F2EFE8`, 1.5 px (1 px for details). No fills except note heads and text.
- Secondary text `#A9A59C`. Staff lines: ink at 35% opacity, 1 px.
- Halos: one faint radial glow per axis, max opacity 0.30, only behind the element it belongs to. Pairs:
  Capture sky `#7FB8E6` to lilac `#A99BE0`; Intelligence lilac to rose `#E59BC0`; Delivery peach `#F2B48C` to coral `#EE7F6E`; Innovation rose to coral.
- Type: Instrument Serif Italic for ONE accent word per phrase (1.35x size) or outlined titles; Inter Tight 300 for phrases (56-76 px at 1080p);
  Geist Mono 500, uppercase, tracking 0.12em, 28 px for labels. Body 32 px Inter Tight 300.

## Metaphor
- A team is a jazz band; each axis is an instrument (Capture = double bass, Intelligence = piano, Delivery = drums, Innovation = saxophone).
- Express concepts as musical notation (notes, chords, accents, beamed eighths on a staff) with mono labels. Never bar charts,
  isometric diagrams or generic line icons. If a concept does not fit the metaphor, use a typographic label.
- Text that shares a slide appears and accumulates in reading order.
- People: black-and-white photo in a circle (220 px) with a thin ring (236 px) and a mono label "NAME · ROLE".

## Motion (video / animated decks)
- 110 BPM, 4/4. Secondary entrances on the "and" of the beat; accents on beats 2 and 4; hard cuts on beat 1. Nothing is ever still:
  slow sway, breathing halos, pulses on 2 and 4. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Rules
- Contrast of text on its background at least 4.5:1. No orbs, no decorative gradients, no stock imagery, no lens flares.
- Do not invent logos, names, photos or figures. Leave a clearly marked placeholder: "place the current logo from the official brand source".
- Do not use client or project names unless the user provides them.
