# Do / Don't and delivery checklist

## Do
- Near-black canvas with grain; one warm-white line; mono labels; one serif accent word per phrase.
- Treat each axis as an instrument and each concept as notation.
- Faint halos tied to one element; generous empty space; reading-order reveals.
- Black-and-white circular photos with a thin ring; placeholders for anything you do not have.
- Keep text at 48 px or more for phrases (1080p) and contrast at 4.5:1 or more.

## Don't
- Bar charts, isometric or 3D diagrams, clip-art or generic line icons, stock imagery, colored photos.
- Gradient backgrounds, orbs, glows used as decoration, drop shadows, lens flares, particle bursts, RGB split, camera shake, crossfades.
- Filled colored shapes; more than one serif word per phrase; all-caps body text; text under 24 px.
- Invented logos, people, quotes, client names, figures. Never ship sample numbers unlabeled.
- Using the ink-faint color (#6F6C66) for text.

## Checklist before delivering
1. Background #0B0B0D with grain; no gradient background.
2. Only #F2EFE8 lines (plus halos). Strokes 1.5 / 1 / 0.66.
3. Fonts: Instrument Serif Italic accent, Inter Tight 300 phrases, Geist Mono 500 uppercase labels.
4. Instruments and notation come from `assets/`; concepts use notation, not charts.
5. Halos at most .30 and tied to an element.
6. Contrast 4.5:1; sizes 48/28/24 px minimum on 1080p.
7. Text accumulates in reading order; motion on the beat grid; nothing still; reduced motion honored.
8. Placeholders clearly marked; logo gap documented; no confidential names.
