# Slides

Canvas 1920x1080 (16:9). Margins 120 px sides, 96 px top, footer baseline 48 px. One idea per slide, flush-left, lots of space.
Start from `templates/slides/index.html` (self-contained, arrow keys). For PowerPoint use the repo's `powerpoint/DT-Jazz.potx`.

## Eight layouts
1. **Cover**: outlined serif title (260 px), a 1.5 px line, mono label (team, project, date), a walking line of notes on the staff.
2. **Section**: instrument left (about 640 px tall) with its halo, mono label "01 - AXIS", one phrase right with a serif accent word, staff at the bottom.
3. **Big phrase**: one or two lines at 104 px, one serif word, small mono eyebrow, short rule.
4. **Person**: B/W circle with ring, mono label "NAME - ROLE", phrase on the right.
5. **Concept as notation**: phrase on top; three chords on the staff with mono labels and 4-6 word captions.
6. **Four axes (the band)**: four columns, an instrument each with its own halo, mono label and one line, a note per column on the staff.
7. **Map / figures**: seeded dots, hubs with ping, big serif numerals with mono captions. Only real figures; sample ones must be marked.
8. **Closing**: outlined serif line, a rule, contact in mono, dashed logo gap with the text "Place the current logo from the official brand source".

## Rules
- Typical order: Cover, Big phrase, Section (x1-4), Four axes, Concept, Figures, Person, Closing. 8-14 slides.
- Text arrives in reading order and accumulates (title -> label -> phrase -> caption); never everything at once.
- Max 12 words per phrase, one serif word. Labels uppercase and short.
- Halos only behind an instrument or a person. At most one halo per element, none on cover/closing text.
- No bars, no isometric, no icons, no stock images. If data is needed use dots, numerals or notation.
- Transitions: hard cut. Animations: fade/rise on the "and" (+273 ms after the beat).
