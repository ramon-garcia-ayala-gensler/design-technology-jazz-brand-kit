# Ready-to-use prompts / Prompts listos

Open `powerpoint/DT-Jazz.potx` first (File > New from template). Replace the text in [brackets]. Works in English or Spanish.

## PowerPoint

1. **Full deck from a topic**
   Create a presentation about [topic] for [audience], 10 slides, using the DT Jazz template layouts. Start with a Cover, include one Section slide per main idea, one Concept in notation slide, a Four axes slide, and a Closing. Keep phrases under 12 words and mark one accent word per phrase.

2. **Outline first, then build**
   Give me a slide-by-slide outline for a [10]-minute talk about [topic]. For each slide: layout, mono label, phrase with one accent word, visual note (instrument, chord, notes), speaker note. Then wait for my feedback before creating slides.

3. **Four axes from my team**
   On the Four axes layout, describe [Capture / Intelligence / Delivery / Innovation, or your own four disciplines] as the double bass, piano, drums and saxophone. One line each, under 8 words, no jargon.

4. **Concept as notation**
   Turn this process into the Concept in notation layout: [steps]. Three steps maximum, each a chord with a mono label (2-3 words) and a caption of 4-6 words.

5. **Rewrite my deck in style**
   Restyle this presentation with the DT Jazz template: keep my content, shorten every headline to at most 12 words, move names and roles into mono labels, remove clip art, bar charts and gradients, and list anything you could not convert.

6. **Speaker notes**
   Write speaker notes for every slide in a warm, confident, plain voice, 40-60 words each, with a cue for when each text line should appear (in reading order).

7. **Person slides**
   Create a Person slide for each of these people: [name - role - one-sentence bio]. Use the round picture placeholder, a mono label with name and role, and do not invent facts.

## Word / Outlook

8. **One-page brief in the same voice**
   Write a one-page brief about [topic] in the DT Jazz voice: plain, warm, confident; short paragraphs; a headline of at most 10 words; three sections with mono-style uppercase subtitles (e.g. CONTEXT, APPROACH, NEXT STEP).

## Designer / Image generation

9. **Poster or social image**
   Create a dark, minimalist line-art image: near-black background #0B0B0D with fine film grain, a single warm-white (#F2EFE8) thin line drawing of a [double bass / piano / drums / saxophone], a very faint radial glow behind it in [sky to lilac / lilac to rose / peach to coral / rose to coral], a five-line staff across the lower third, generous empty space on the right. No gradients elsewhere, no text, no people, no 3D.

10. **Check my slide against the style**
    Review this slide against the DT Jazz rules (dark background, one thin line, mono labels, one serif accent word, notation instead of charts, faint halos only, text contrast 4.5:1). List violations and propose fixes.
