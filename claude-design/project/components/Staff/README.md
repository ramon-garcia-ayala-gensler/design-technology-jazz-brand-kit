# Staff

Five horizontal lines that carry notation. The stage floor of every composition.

- 5 lines, 24 px apart on a 1080p canvas, 1 px stroke, ink at 35% opacity (token `staff`).
- Usually across the full width in the lower third (y 820 to 916 at 1080p). Notes and instruments stand on it.
- Optional very faint wave (about 2 px) crossing once per bar in motion.
- Asset: `assets/notation/staff.svg` in the repo.
